Program

Seaside Logistics Driver Safety Alerts. Seaside Logistics Inc sends operational safety text messages, including daily weather maps, road condition alerts and safety reminders, to its employed and contracted truck drivers who have given written consent during onboarding.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies, usually 1 to 2 messages per day.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt out

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. You will receive one final message confirming you have been unsubscribed.

Help

Reply HELP for help, or contact your Seaside Logistics dispatcher.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
